1. Knee Joint 
================

- i2c address : 0x40
- Encoder convention: Flexion negative as well as extension negative  --- from the home position 
- Magnetic Encoder offset of knee = 120.0 degrees
- The sign convention of knee dataset and knee encoder is same
- The sign convention of knee encoder and knee motor is opposite

2. Ankle Joint
=================

- i2c address: 0x41
- Encoder convention: Dorsiflexion Negative and Plantarflexion positive --- from the mean postion.
- Magnetic Encoder offset of the ankle joint at its mean postion = 200.0
- The sign convention of ankle dataset and knee encoder is opposite
- The sign convention of ankle encoder and ankle motor is opposite
